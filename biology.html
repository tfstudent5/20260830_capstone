What is biology

Biology is the study of life, from the smallest cells to entire ecosystesm. It explains how living things grow, adapt, reproduce and interact with their environment. It gives us a framework for understanding everything that lives, how it works and how all forms of life are connected.

Four parts of biology

Botany

Botany studies plants, how they grow and how they interact with their environment.

Zoology

Zoology focuses on animals, their behaviours, structure and evolution.

Microbiology

Microbiology examines microscopic life such as bacteria, viruses and fungi

Ecology

Ecology explores how living things interact with each other and the world around them.
