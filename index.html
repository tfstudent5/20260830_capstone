Science is humanity's way of listening to the world; by observing, questioning, and testing ideas to reveal the order and beauty beneath. We study Chemistry, Biology, and Physics because each examines the world from a different perspective: matter, life, and the principles that govern them. Together they give us a clearer, more organised way to understand how everything fits together. Click below to learn more about them.

Physics

The study of matter, motion, and energy; the principles that explain how the universe behaves.

Chemistry

The study of matter and its transformations: how substances change, combine, and react to shape the world.

Biology

The study of life: how living things grow, adapt, and sustain themselves in an ever changing world.
